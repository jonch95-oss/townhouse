# Notice — what this material is and how it may be used

This folder is a **specification** of the structure, motion and interaction design of
https://quadplex80.com, produced as reference for building an equivalent experience for
223 Waverly Avenue, Brooklyn.

## Reference-only material

Captured alongside the specification, and included **solely so a developer can check
layout, proportion and timing**, are:

- screenshots and frame sequences of the live site
- the site's stylesheet and JavaScript bundles
- the decoded CMS payload, which contains the site's marketing copy
- a manifest listing (but not containing) its image and video assets

None of this is packaged for reuse and **none of it should ship on 223 Waverly Avenue.**
The photography, copy, logotype and marks belong to 111 West 57th Street and its partners
(JDS Development Group, Property Markets Group, SHoP, Studio Sofield, Sotheby's
International Realty, Hayes Davidson). The site's design and build are credited to
Outpost (outpost.design).

No imagery, video, font file or line of their copy is redistributed here. The bundles are
included because reading them is how the timings in `motion-spec.md` were established, and
a developer may want to verify a number against the source.

## What actually transfers

Proportions, durations, easing curves, the interaction model, and the restraint. All of
that is described in prose and numbers, and all of it is legitimate to rebuild — which is
the point of a specification rather than a scrape.

Copy for 223 Waverly must be **written, not adapted**. The verbatim quotes in
`sections.md` are another building's words about another building; what transfers is the
discipline behind them — roughly ninety words across the whole experience, and nothing at
all on the floor slides.
