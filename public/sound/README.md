Warm late-light lounge bed (felt piano, nylon guitar, soft pulse). Chosen as a free
stand-in for a “Sundown”-style vibe; Gin & Tonic’s *Sundown* is not free to use.

Volumes stay low in `src/lib/sound.ts`. Visitors can mute via Sound, or enter with
“Enter without sound”. Credit also appears in the site Credits panel.
