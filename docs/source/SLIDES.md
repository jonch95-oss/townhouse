# Slide structure — rooms, not floors

The plan sheets (GC set A-100.01 / A-101.01) give the real programme, and it does not match
the assumption I was working from earlier:

  1st floor  foyer · library · courtyard · hallway · KITCHEN · DINING ROOM · bath
  2nd floor  bedroom 1 · bath · hallway · LIVING ROOM · terrace (93.32 sf)
  3rd floor  primary bedroom · primary bathroom · W.I.C. · dressing room · terrace
  4th floor  bedroom · bath · hallway · terrace · mechanical
  roof       two roof terraces (164.38 sf, 220.97 sf) · vestibule · mechanical

The kitchen is on the GROUND floor. Earlier guidance placed it on the 2nd. Anything already
labelled that way is factually wrong about the building and must be corrected.

## Consequence

Organising slides by floor would require imagery for four floors. Renders exist for three
rooms, and two of those are on the same floor. So the slides are organised BY ROOM:

  gate      terracotta and brick        landscape/gate.jpg      (powder room)
  slide 0   hero                        landscape/00-hero.jpg
  slide 1   Entrance & Library          landscape/01-*.jpg      1st floor
  slide 2   Living                      landscape/02-*.jpg      1st floor kitchen/dining
  slide 3   Primary Suite               landscape/03-*.jpg      3rd floor
  slide 4   Contact                     no image

Five slides. Every one has a real image and a truthful label. No slide claims a floor it
isn't on.

If floor labels are wanted anywhere, they belong in the floorplans panel where the plan
itself is the evidence.
