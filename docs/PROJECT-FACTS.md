## Construction & finishes (from wall types / details)
- **Glen Gery Potenza Raw brick veneer** with vertical/corbelled coursing
- **SK1N Terra PG terracotta cladding** (rainscreen panel system)
- Aluminum coping, sills, window extrusions and canopy in **Terra Brown RAL 8028**
- Stucco at rear
- Sculpted facade profile with a curved parapet and a **4'-0" diameter round window**
- 1-hr rated exterior assemblies (UL H505), STC 50 / IIC 50
- 2" continuous XPS rigid insulation + batt; calculated U-value 0.048
- Floor assembly: 5/8" hardwood finish / plywood / **Ecore QT acoustic underlayment**
  / Homasote 440 soundbarrier / VersaRoc — i.e. an acoustically isolated floor
- Concrete pavers on adjustable pedestals at terraces; 4" roof water detention
- Windows: trickle-vented (Titan), sound attenuators, incl. a **4'-0" diameter
  round window (W6)** and 3'-0" x 8'-0" full-height fixed units (W1A)
- Stair: walnut rounded cap handrail, 3/4" round wood balusters
- 42" guardrails throughout; 38" stair handrail
- High-efficacy lighting per TPPN 2020-008
- Elevator detail sheet omitted from the GC amendment; client confirmed no elevator

## Interior design direction (from the two StudioSC design decks)
Warm minimalism — limewash/plaster walls in bone and sand, curved wall returns
and arched openings, light rift-oak millwork with recessed pulls, fluted-glass
upper cabinets, book-matched green-veined marble (Calacatta Viola / Verde style)
on kitchen counters, island and full-height backsplash, plaster range hood,
wide-plank pale oak floors, terracotta/brick-toned patterned tile at the entry,
bouclé and cream upholstery, red-marble accent pieces.
